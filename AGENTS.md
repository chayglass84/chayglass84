## Kong Insomnia: Senior Staff Product Manager (Apr 2025 to present)

Sole PM for Insomnia, Kong's API client. I drove adding AI and MCP (Model Context Protocol) capabilities to it. About 150K weekly active users and $9M ARR. I own strategy, vision and roadmap.

**Context.** I inherited an immature product-led growth (PLG) product and millions of dollars in high-risk pipeline it did not yet fully serve. The job was to protect that pipeline, then find where Insomnia could win.

- **9X ARR ($1M to $9M) in 18 months.** Repositioned Insomnia around a native Kong gateway integration: the only tool giving enterprise developers (and their agents) secure, always-current access to every endpoint. One of my four 0->1 wins.
- **Strategy alignment in 3 weeks.** Used AI-assisted working prototypes instead of PRDs. Tested competing bets in parallel over 2 months: an agent-friendly CLI, an MCP client and the gateway integration. Committed to the gateway integration. Prototypes are in my public repos (see Projects).
- **Identified governance and compliance as Insomnia's sustained unfair advantage.** Insomnia does not hold customer data, which sets it apart from Postman. Customers choose where their data goes, for AI and Git alike. This is the core of the enterprise story in regulated industries.
- **Fixed the product without a rewrite.** Rebuilt the highest-risk pieces, Git Sync, SSO/SCIM and core UX, in 8 months while holding the enterprise base. 2025 results: 112% net dollar retention, 98% enterprise retention.
- **Tripled active enterprise-seat usage in 12 months.** I built the activation motion from zero (docs, onboarding, cadences with support, success and professional services). The metric: of all sold enterprise seats, how many had an active user in the last 30 days.
- **Focused go-to-market (GTM) on where Insomnia could win.** Commoditized Git Sync by making it free, against Postman's paywall. Worked with product marketing on PLG and enterprise campaigns. Signups grew 17% in 2025.
- **Redefined how we measure success.** Replaced overcounting metrics with a full-funnel scorecard: signups, 14-day activation, WAU, product-qualified leads. I drove monthly business reviews to align the team and execs on it. Added CSAT for qualitative feedback. Executed with a data hire.
- **Found and validated $20-50M of enterprise expansion potential.** It came from open-source-based PQLs. Built the motion end to end: narrative, field enablement, exec alignment, Salesforce targeting.

## Customer.io: Senior Product Manager (May 2022 to Nov 2024)

Owned core integrations and data modeling, helping marketers turn real-time data into precise audiences.

- **First paid feature: whiteboard sketch to $3M net new ARR.** Customer.io could model only people. B2B and marketplace customers needed companies, objects and events too (for example, notify the 20 people watching an auction item when it gets a bid, or onboard 50 employees when their company buys a plan). Customer.io charges by the number of profiles it tracks, so I built the feature into that model: more tracked entities meant more revenue. Adopted by 11% of clients within a year, with adoption up 240% in 2023. In 2024 I drove a repackaging of the feature's pricing, from two profile buckets (people and objects) to one unified bucket where every profile is a profile, to make it easier to adopt. One of my four 0->1 wins.
- **Strategic pivot toward less technical midmarket users.** Customer interviews showed our buyer had shifted toward non-technical marketers as we went upmarket, and advanced features were overwhelming even our champions. Usage data agreed. I got CEO alignment, then led a shift to guided UX: from an early AI feature that generated Liquid syntax from plain language, to a rebuilt workflow creation experience. Email campaign activation went from 87% to 97% in 3 months.
- **Enablement.** CSMs and sales engineers could not demo our most complex features. I built tutorials, videos and a monthly enablement cadence. Adoption climbed across the features it touched.
- **Built a best-in-class bidirectional Salesforce integration in martech: 30+ enterprise clients and $300K ARR in the first 3 months.** Took over the project in crisis the week it was supposed to ship, cut scope to what enterprise customers needed most and hit key deadlines.
- **CDP.** Joint product manager and engineering manager for the CDP (a lightweight Segment or mParticle) for about 3 months, alongside the work above.

## UnionWare: Head of Product (Feb 2017 to Jan 2022)

Enterprise software for labor and trade unions. I led product and design, managed a team of 10 (including engineers), and owned strategy, vision, lifecycle and go-to-market.

- **Strategy.** Focused the roadmap on helping clients through the Right-to-Work era. The company grew 50% in clients and 52% in revenue from 2017 to 2021.
- **Direct-member payment platform.** Right-to-Work laws stop unions from collecting dues through payroll, so they must collect directly from individual members. That was existential for about half our clients. After a private-equity acquisition in 2018, I led product, design and GTM for a new platform (design and go-to-market did not exist as functions before). Launched January 2020. About 30% client adoption within 18 months, $1M+ ARR, and the best profit margin across the parent company's 30-company portfolio. One of my four 0->1 wins.
- **Organizing product.** Organizing is sales and marketing for unions. I spent time with customers in the field, ran them as an ongoing focus group, iterated weekly and shipped to a real customer in 4 months. Adopted by 40% of clients in 2 years, $1M+ ARR. One of my four 0->1 wins.
- **First view of product usage.** Built the company's first usage-measurement system. Every product had its own database schema, so cross-product usage was invisible. It let us see which bets were working and redirect over $2M toward the strategy above.
- **Saved $300-500K a year with a Solution Architecture program.** A cross-functional program to close gaps between client needs and platform capabilities. Also cut project build time by 20%+.
- **Closed over $1M in new ARR representing UnionWare at 40+ events over a decade.** Evangelism, sales support, marketing and training.
